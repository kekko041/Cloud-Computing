# English conversation — DTD1 oral exam (level B1)

> Bando, art. 8 c.4 lett. c: *"conversazione che accerti le competenze linguistiche di livello almeno B1"*. Allo scritto hai fatto **7/8**. All'orale serve **fluidità**, non perfezione. Materiale complementare: `MIT/orale/01_english_conversation_prep.md` (più tecnico, livello B2).

## How to handle it
- Answer in **3-4 simple sentences**: point → reason → example.
- If you don't understand: *"Sorry, could you repeat the question, please?"* / *"Do you mean…?"*
- If you need time: *"That's a good question. Let me think…"*
- If you lose a word: describe it (*"the system that checks the identity of citizens… SPID"*).

## 1. About you (prepare it, ~45 seconds)
> *"My name is Santo Penna. I work as [role] at [organisation], where I deal with [IT projects / networks / security]. I have [N] years of experience in the public sector. I applied for this position because I would like to help public administrations become more digital, and the Department for Digital Transformation is the place where national digital strategy becomes real projects."*

## 2. Likely questions with model answers
**What does the Department for Digital Transformation do?**
> *"It supports the Prime Minister in defining and coordinating the national strategy for digital transformation. It also manages the digital investments of the Recovery Plan, for example cloud migration for municipalities, digital identity, the pagoPA payment platform and the IO app."*

**What is cloud computing and why is it useful for a municipality?**
> *"Cloud computing means using IT resources, like servers and software, over the internet and paying only for what you use. For a small municipality it is useful because it improves security and reliability, and the staff don't need to manage their own data centre."*

**How do you protect personal data?**
> *"First, we collect only the data we really need. Then we protect it with access control, encryption and backups. Finally, we train people, because many attacks start with a phishing email."*

**Describe a project you worked on.** Usa lo STAR in inglese: *"The situation was… My task was to… I decided to… As a result, …"*

**What are the advantages and risks of artificial intelligence in public administration?**
> *"AI can reduce repetitive work and help citizens get information faster, for example with a chatbot. However, there are risks, like wrong answers, bias and privacy issues. So humans must always check the important decisions."*

**Do you prefer working alone or in a team? / How do you handle stress?**
> *"I like working in a team, because different skills give better results. When there is pressure, I set priorities, I break the work into small tasks and I communicate early if there is a problem."*

## 3. Useful phrases
| Function | Phrases |
|---|---|
| Opinion | *In my opinion… / I think that… / From my point of view…* |
| Sequence | *First… Then… After that… Finally…* |
| Contrast | *However… / On the other hand… / Although…* |
| Example | *For example… / For instance… / such as…* |
| Cause and effect | *because… / so… / as a result… / that's why…* |
| Conclusion | *To sum up… / In short…* |
